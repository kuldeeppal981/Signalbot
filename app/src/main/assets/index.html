About: strategy and calculations

Educational tool, not investment advice. No accuracy is claimed. Options buying is high risk and you can lose the whole premium. The edge of this strategy is NOT proven; validate with Backtest and paper trading first.

1. What the app does

Nifty and Bank Nifty, CE or PE BUYING only, ATM or 1-strike OTM. Strict signals (Tier A): max 2 per day, and "No Trade" when unclear. Data: Upstox (main), Angel One (optional spot cross-check).

2. Signal: 6-factor score (5-minute candles)

Each factor is +1 (bullish), -1 (bearish) or 0:
1. Spot vs 5m EMA20
2. EMA9 vs EMA20 (5m)
3. Opening range breakout (9:15-9:30 high/low, 0.03% buffer)
4. 15m trend vs 15m EMA20
5. PCR: above 1.1 bullish, below 0.8 bearish
6. 3-candle momentum
EMAs are warmed up with the previous sessions (days are never merged).
Tier A signal: |score| ≥ 4 AND opening-range break agrees AND spot-vs-EMA20 agrees. Positive = CE, negative = PE.
Tier B ("setup forming"): |score| ≥ 3 AND spot-vs-EMA20 agrees. Max 5 a day. PAPER ONLY, never a real-trade signal. It exists to learn whether a lower threshold would work.
Setup strength 0-100: score x 10, +15 opening-range agrees, +10 15m trend agrees, +5 PCR agrees, +5 VIX ≤ 18, +5 trending market. It is NOT a win probability.

3. Gates (any one fails = No Trade)

Time window 9:30-14:30. Max 2 Tier A signals a day. No stacking while a trade is open. 15-min cooldown after a loss; stop after 2 losses in a row. Daily loss limit = 2 x risk per trade (Zero-Hero worst case included). Expiry day: no fresh buys after 1:30 PM. India VIX ≤ 24. Angel vs Upstox spot difference ≤ 0.1%. Duplicate-direction signal blocked for 30 min, opposite direction for 20 min. Data health check.
Also: price not too far from EMA20 (0.5% Nifty / 0.7% Bank Nifty), no big OI wall within 0.3% of spot, expiry pin check (0.08%), IV percentile ≤ 90, delta ≥ 0.40 (0.45 near expiry), bid-ask spread ≤ 5%.
Optional filters (Settings, off by default): breakout confirmation, sideways filter, retest of the opening range. Turn on only if the A/B test helps.

4. Position size (the calculation)

Risk budget = capital x risk % (default 1%, max 5%)
Risk per lot = 0.25 x option premium x lot size (because SL is -25%)
Lots = floor(risk budget / risk per lot)
If lots = 0 (even 1 lot risks more than your budget) = No Trade. The budget is never exceeded.
Example: capital Rs 3,00,000, risk 1% = budget Rs 3,000. Premium 110, lot 75: risk per lot = 0.25 x 110 x 75 = Rs 2,062, so 1 lot.
With small capital (Rs 5k-30k) 1 lot often risks more than the budget, so the app skips. Forcing a trade means over-risking.

5. Trade levels

Entry zone: LTP to LTP x 1.02 (do not chase).
Stop loss: -25% of entry premium.
Target 1: +50%. Target 2: +75%. (Risk:reward 1:2 at T1.)
Index invalidation: exit early if the index closes beyond EMA20 or the extreme of the last 3 candles against you.
Time exit: 45 minutes after entry if neither SL nor target is hit.
1 lot: exit fully at T1. 2+ lots: sell half (whole lots) at T1, move the stop to entry, rest to T2.
Example: entry 100 gives SL 75, T1 150, T2 175. 1 lot of 75: loss at SL = Rs 1,875, gain at T1 = Rs 3,750 (before charges).

6. Zero-Hero (expiry day only, Off by default)

1:00-2:45 PM, cheap far-OTM option, notification only, one per day, budget default 1% of capital, counted in the daily risk limit. Very high risk, it usually expires worthless.

7. Paper trading

A virtual trade starts when a signal appears. States: pending, open, partial, closed, missed. Fills at the ask, exits at the bid, charges included. Shadow stops (25/33/40%) are watched for 45 min to learn if the stop is too tight. Tier A and Tier B are tracked separately.

8. Backtest

Index level only (no option premium, no PCR). Entry at the next candle open, stop at invalidation (min 0.12%), target 2R, stop first if both hit in one candle, time stop 45 min, end of day 15:15. Results in R. The verdict uses the realised break-even win rate and the expectancy with its error range. 100+ trades are needed before judging.

8b. Trade Watcher (real trades)

When you tap "I took this trade", the Journal trade is watched every minute (auto-scan on). Alerts: index crossed the invalidation level (exit now), stop-loss hit, fast premium drop (-12% in 5 min), Target 1 (book, move the stop to entry), breakeven stop after T1, no progress at 40 min, time exit at 45 min, market closing. It never places or changes orders. Put a stop-loss order at your broker right after entry: that is the real protection. You can enter your real fill price and the levels are recalculated.

9. Learning report and AI agent

Journal tab: the Learning report finds patterns (time, CE/PE, score, VIX, execution, mistakes) and gives suggestions with sample sizes. It never changes the strategy by itself. The optional AI second-opinion agent (Settings) reviews a Tier A signal and says TAKE / WAIT / SKIP. It is advisory only: it cannot change size, stop-loss or place orders, and its opinions are compared with results in the report.

10. Honest limits

The backtest covers only the index core; option-level filters are tested by paper trading. Small samples are mostly luck. Backtest is not live: slippage, psychology and market changes matter. Paper trade for 4-12 weeks before real money. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
